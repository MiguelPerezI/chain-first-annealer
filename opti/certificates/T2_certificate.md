## How it was obtained

1. The chain‑first GPU annealer (`gpu_sc.py`, Kernel A, with the corrected energy `beta 5, gamma 0.5, ptrack 0.3` of
   `optimized_algorithms.md` §14) was run on the torus with 3–4 chains (§15). Every 216‑facet domain it produced turned out to
   be a product `star(v) × star(v)` of a closed vertex star with itself, with a 2‑link chain `π₁ ∼ c_v ∼ π₂` (§16.1).
2. This was turned into the explicit construction below (valid for any complex), so the covering problem became a set‑cover
   problem over the 49 rectangles `star(v) × star(w)`; its exact optimum, 11 rectangles, was computed with an integer program
   (`scipy.optimize.milp`, HiGHS; a branch‑and‑bound in `rect_cover.py` independently excludes ≤ 9). The greedy cover has 13.
3. `star_cover.py` builds the 11 domains and chains, shortens the chains with Algorithm 2 of the paper (`reduce_chain`), verifies
   the system with the plain Definition 2.3 check (`gpu_sc.verify_system`) and writes `cover.txt` / `planners.txt` /
   `summary.json`; `Abstract_Motion_GPU/independent_check.py` (code‑independent auditor) re‑derives the torus, the product and
   every chain and exits 0.

Attempts to do better than 11 by search (whole‑cover annealing with 3–11 chains, hybrids of fixed rectangles plus annealed
chains, translation‑invariant ansätze, diagonal bands) all failed; see `optimized_algorithms.md` §15–16.

## The construction

For vertices `v, w` of `K`, `D(v, w) = star(v) × star(w)` = all facets over pairs of triangles `(s, t)` with `v ∈ s`, `w ∈ t` (36 pairs, 216 facets).
It is a contiguity domain with the chain `π₁ ∼ c_v ∼ c_w ∼ π₂` (`c_x` = constant map to `x`; two links if `v = w`, since `π₁(σ) ∪ c_v(σ) = s`,
`c_v(σ) ∪ c_w(σ) = {v, w}` is an edge of `K₇`, `c_w(σ) ∪ π₂(σ) = t`). The minimum number of such rectangles covering all 196 pairs is 11
(integer program, `rect_cover.py` / `run_torus.sh ilp`); the parts below are the rectangles with facets already covered by an earlier rectangle removed.
